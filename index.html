Shipping apps people actually use

I'm a Flutter Developer from Sonipat, Haryana who loves turning product ideas into fast, polished mobile experiences on both Android and iOS from a single codebase.

Over the last three years I've built a video management system streaming live CCTV feeds, an HRMS with GPS attendance and offline sync, embedded a Flutter module inside a native Android app used by thousands of aspirants, and I'm now building consumer features at PinnTag with over-the-air updates via Shorebird.

My toolkit centres on Bloc, GetX, Clean Architecture and Firebase, with a strong focus on offline-first data using Sqflite and Hive.
