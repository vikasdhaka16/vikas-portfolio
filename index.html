01 · about

Shipping apps people actually use

I'm a Flutter Developer from Haryana who loves turning product ideas into fast, polished mobile experiences on Android and iOS from a single codebase.

Over the last three years I've built a video management system streaming live CCTV feeds, an HRMS with GPS attendance and offline sync, a restaurant ordering app with Razorpay payments, and embedded a Flutter module inside the live native VisionIAS Android app using FlutterFragment.

My toolkit centres on Bloc, Cubit, GetX, Clean Architecture and Firebase, with a strong focus on offline-first data using Sqflite and Hive — plus over-the-air updates with Shorebird.
